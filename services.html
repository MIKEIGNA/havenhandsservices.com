Our Services

Professional staff for your home

House Manager

Someone to keep your home running - cooking, cleaning, laundry, grocery shopping, and general upkeep. Trained on modern appliances, kitchen hygiene, and proper cleaning methods.

Nannies & Babysitters

Staff who genuinely enjoy working with children. They handle school runs, homework, meals, and keep your kids safe and happy. Trained in first aid and early childhood basics.

Elderly & Companion Care

Patient, compassionate people for aging parents or grandparents. They help with medication reminders, mobility, light housekeeping, meals, and companionship.

Newborn & Postnatal Care

Specialized support for new mothers and newborns - feeding assistance, bath time, sleep routines, and postnatal recovery care.

Drivers

Reliable, licensed drivers for school runs, errands, and family transport. Trained in road safety and defensive driving.

Gardeners

Skilled gardeners for lawn care, landscaping, plant maintenance, and keeping your outdoor spaces looking their best.

Special Needs Care

Compassionate, trained staff for children or adults with special needs - patient, skilled, and dedicated to providing quality support.

Daybugs (Part-Time Help)

Flexible part-time help for specific tasks - cleaning, cooking, laundry, or occasional housekeeping when you need it.

Pet Sitters

Reliable pet care while you're away - feeding, walking, grooming, and keeping your furry friends happy and safe.
